The first machine that assisted people in calculations was the abacus. In 1642 Blaise Pascal, a french mathmatician, invented an adding machine based on mechanical gears and the numbers were represented by the cogs on the wheel.
But, the first machine that have the key characteristics of modern computer was the Difference Engine, invented in 1830's by Charles Babbage. The Difference Engine contained an input device, a store for numbers waiting to be processed, a processor, a unit to control the task and an output device.
In 1890 the first electrically drive device was developed. It utilised punched cards and metal rods which passed through the holes to close an electric circuit and thus case a counter to advance. In 1936, IBM invested 1 million dollars to develop an electromechanical version of the Difference Engine and in 1944 was completed and at about the same time the first digital computer that worked electronically was built.
In 1943, during the Second World War, a series of vacuum tbe based computers named Colossus were developed to crack German secret codes. Two professors from the University of Pennsylvania devoloped the idea of the vacuum based computer further by proposing a machine consisting of 18,000 vacuum tubes. ENIAC was born in 1946, a huge machine that required a huge power and maintenance was very difficult.
Some years later, John Von Neumann developed the EDVAC which pioneered the stored program concept and this allowed to give birht to the age of general-purpose compters.
There are five generation of computers:
	- the first generation started with the delivery of the first commercial computer to a business client and this happend in 1951 and this generation lasted until the end of the 1950's and the main feature was that vacuum tubes were used an internal computer component;
	- the second generation gave birth to the transistor that were capable of performing many task as vacuum tabes but were a fraction of the size. The first transistor-based computer was developed in 1959. With transistors, computers were smaller and faster and consumed less electricity;
	- the third generation saw the development of the first integrated circit where a complete circuit was able to be placed on a single silicon chip. The computers that used IC replaced the transistor-based machines because they were more powerful, smaller and cheaper.
	Another feature of this generation is that softwares became more powerful and flexible and for the first time one program could share the computer's resources.
	-  the boundary between third and fourth generation is nto very clear. In 1970 large-scale integration was aschieved, so thousand of integrated circuits were placed on a single silicon chip. Aroun d this time the first general-purpose microprocessor became avaible on a single chip and in 1975 with Very Large Scale Integration the first microcomputer was born.
	- the figth generation is the future. 5G machine incorporate the features of AI, Expert Systems and Natural Language. The goal is to produce machine capable of performing task similar to huumans, capable of learning and capable of interating with hmans.
