The intense use of internet and social media can lead to digital addiction. Suffering from digital addiction means feeling worse and worse when you spend a lot of time on social media. According to some studies, around five billlion people of the eight billion people on the planet are connected to the Internet and spend an average of almost 7 hours online. But the bad thing of this is that there is a connection between heavy internet use and feelings of depression and other mental problems.
The term 'digital addiction' was used by Dr. Gregory Jantz to describe a compulsion to se any form of digital media and he treats digital addictions as a real condition.
Dr. Jantz claims that most of the people know to have a problem related to the use of digital media but they don't consider it as an addiction. Dr. Jantz, when treating his patients, take away anything with a screen and some days later the patients begin to give states of addiction. They start asking back for a device, they have headache, sweaty palms, the heart rate goes up and so on.
Dr. Jantz describe digital media as the cocaine of online because digital media are built in such a way that they keep people always looking. Moreover, most people underestimate how much time they spend online and they don't track how much time they spend on it.
Dr. Jantz says that you have to ask yourself a series of question to understand if you suffer from digital addiction. Some of the questions may be if you can control how much time you spend on it, what are you really using it for, if you can do a day a week away from social media. Maybe digital addiction may be treated as a real addiction in the future and at some point there could be a diagnostic code for it.
